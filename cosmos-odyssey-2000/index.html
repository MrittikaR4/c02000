01 — OVERVIEW

Space exploration is a system of decisions.

COSMOS: ODYSSEY 2000 is an educational mission-design concept that turns the complexity of space exploration into an interactive learning experience. Players work with scientific goals, spacecraft systems, launch options, power, mass, communication, and mission constraints to plan a mission from concept to outcome.
