01 / THE CONCEPT

A universe of choices.
A mission of consequences.

COSMOS: ODYSSEY 2000 is an educational space mission design game concept built around exploration, scientific reasoning and engineering trade-offs.

Players take on the challenge of planning a space mission. Every decision has a purpose: the destination determines the journey, the instruments determine what can be discovered, and the spacecraft must operate within practical limits.

The objective is not simply to reach another world. It is to understand why successful missions depend on careful planning, reliable systems and evidence-based decisions.

03 / THE MISSION LOOP

Plan. Test. Adapt.

The proposed gameplay follows the same broad logic used in mission planning: define the goal, build a system, evaluate constraints and revise the design.

01

Define the objective

Choose a target and establish what the mission should discover.

02

Build the spacecraft

Choose the instruments and systems needed to achieve the goal.

03

Evaluate constraints

Review mass, power, cost, communication and destination requirements.

04

Simulate the outcome

Assess the mission against its objectives and learn from the results.
